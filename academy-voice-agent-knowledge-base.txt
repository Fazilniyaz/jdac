JADVIX ACADEMY — VOICE AGENT KNOWLEDGE BASE
============================================

Purpose: factual reference for the Jadvix Academy voice assistant. These are
the facts the agent may state on a call. Fees and policy come from the Academy
single source of truth. If a caller asks something not covered here, the agent
says it is not certain and offers an advisor callback — it does not invent.

Last updated: 2026-10-11 (keep in sync with the website content).


1. THE COMPANY
--------------
- Legal name: Jadvix LTD (a UK software company).
- Training arm: Jadvix Academy.
- Locations: Ruislip, United Kingdom; Chennai, India; Coimbatore, India.
- Delivery of the course is run from Chennai and Coimbatore, with Jadvix LTD
  in the UK.


2. THE PROGRAM (OVERVIEW)
-------------------------
- One program: "Full Stack Development with Agentic AI".
- Format: a 10-month, milestone-gated, train-to-hire program.
- Delivery: hybrid — online and offline. A student can take part from Chennai,
  Coimbatore, or remotely.
- Three stages, in order:
    Stage 1 — Frontend Development   (Months 1–4)
    Stage 2 — Backend Development    (Months 5–8)
    Stage 3 — Agentic AI             (Months 9–10)
- Each stage ends in an evaluation the student must clear to unlock the next
  stage. Clearing a stage also earns a matching 6-month PAID internship.
- AI-assisted engineering with Claude Code runs through the whole program —
  it is built in, not an add-on.
- Learning is by building: guided, client-style project work and real
  code-review cycles, not passive lectures.
- Every cohort has two coordinators:
    Technical Coordinator — curriculum delivery, code reviews, gate-test
      design and evaluation.
    English Coordinator — professional communication, client-facing
      readiness, spoken and written English coaching.
- Certificate: UK-issued Jadvix Academy certificate.

KEY STATS
- 10 months of structured training.
- 6 months of paid internship (per stage cleared).
- Hybrid (online & offline) delivery.
- UK-issued Jadvix Academy certificate.


3. THE STAGES AND GATES
-----------------------
Stage 1 — Frontend Development (Months 1–4)
  Summary: core web, React, and AI-assisted engineering with Claude Code.
  Evaluation: Frontend Evaluation at the end of Month 4.
  On pass: Frontend Development Intern offer; cleared for Stage 2.
  On fail: continue Stage 1; retry after 2 weeks (maximum 2 attempts).

Stage 2 — Backend Development (Months 5–8)
  Summary: server-side fundamentals, data, and full stack integration with
  the student's Stage 1 work.
  Evaluation: Backend Evaluation — TWO parts, both must be cleared:
    - Practical test: a timed, hands-on backend test set by the Technical
      Coordinator.
    - Full stack project: an individually assigned project (assigned to every
      student) integrating Stage 1 and Stage 2 work.
  When: end of Month 8.
  On pass: Backend Development Intern offer; cleared for Stage 3.
  On fail: continue Stage 2; retry after 2 weeks (maximum 2 attempts).

Stage 3 — Agentic AI (Months 9–10)
  Summary: LLM-powered agents, tool use, and shipping a production-style
  agentic capstone.
  Evaluation: Agentic AI Capstone at the end of Month 10 — a production-style
  agentic feature reviewed against Jadvix engineering standards.
  On pass: Agentic AI Development Intern offer (6 months).
  On fail: continue Stage 3; retry after 2 weeks (maximum 2 attempts).

SEQUENTIAL RULE
- Every evaluation must be cleared to unlock the next stage.
- Backend cannot begin until the Frontend evaluation is cleared.
- Agentic AI cannot begin until the Backend evaluation (test AND project) is
  cleared.
- The same sequential rule applies to lateral (single-stage) entry.
- Each pass unlocks the matching 6-month paid internship.

RETRY POLICY
- A student who fails an evaluation stays in their current stage and keeps
  attending coursework.
- They may retry after a 2-week cooling-off period.
- Maximum of 2 attempts.
- NOTE (not yet decided): what happens after both retry attempts are used up
  is still being finalised. The agent must NOT invent an answer — offer an
  advisor callback.


4. CURRICULUM (WHAT IS TAUGHT)
------------------------------
Stage 1 — Frontend (Months 1–4)
  - Core Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript ES6+, responsive design, Git/GitHub
    workflow.
  - Modern Framework: React.js — components, hooks, state management, routing.
  - AI-Assisted Engineering: frontend enhancement and productivity workflows
    using Claude Code.
  - Applied Practice: guided client-style project builds and code-review cycles.

Stage 2 — Backend (Months 5–8)
  - Server-Side Fundamentals: Node.js / Express or equivalent, REST API design,
    authentication & authorization.
  - Data: SQL & NoSQL design, query optimization, data modelling.
  - Integration: full stack integration with Stage 1 frontend projects,
    deployment basics.
  - Applied Practice: end-to-end feature delivery under code review.
  - Evaluation Project: an individually assigned full stack project.

Stage 3 — Agentic AI (Months 9–10)
  - Agentic Systems: LLM-powered agents, tool use, workflow automation.
  - Applied AI Engineering: building and shipping agentic features into full
    stack apps.
  - Capstone: a production-style agentic feature reviewed against Jadvix
    engineering standards.

Technologies covered across the program: HTML & CSS, JavaScript, React,
Node.js, REST APIs, SQL & NoSQL, Git & GitHub, Claude Code, Agentic AI, Full
Stack.

Supporting tracks (all stages): Technical Coordinator track and English
Coordinator track (see section 2).


5. INTERNSHIP
-------------
- Length: 6 months per internship (one per stage cleared).
- Stipend: PAID, as per Jadvix's internship policy. DO NOT state an amount —
  the amount is intentionally not published.
- Work: interns work on live or client-adjacent projects under the supervision
  of the Technical Coordinator and an assigned engineering lead.
- Parallel coursework: Frontend and Backend interns may continue coursework in
  parallel with their internship.
- Terms: interns sign standard confidentiality and IP assignment terms before
  starting.
- Capacity: internship offers are capped by Jadvix LTD's confirmed intern
  capacity per cohort. Clearing an evaluation keeps a student eligible but does
  NOT guarantee a place.

INTERN ROLES BY STAGE
- Stage 1 cleared -> Frontend Development Intern.
- Stage 2 cleared -> Backend Development Intern.
- Stage 3 cleared -> Agentic AI Development Intern.


6. THE PATHWAY TO EMPLOYMENT (IMPORTANT WORDING)
------------------------------------------------
- The program is "a direct pathway to full-time employment at Jadvix LTD"
  after the 6-month internship.
- It is subject to clearing each evaluation AND to Jadvix LTD's confirmed
  intern capacity per cohort.
- This is NEVER a guaranteed job. The agent must always describe it as a
  pathway, never a guarantee.
- Conversion to full-time (all three tracks) is based on: code-review scores,
  sprint/task completion rate, reliability, and coordinator sign-off — leading
  to a full-time offer at Jadvix LTD.

COMPLETION REWARD
- Students who pass the Agentic AI capstone AND convert to full-time employment
  receive:
    - a 1 gram gold coin, and
    - a UK-issued Jadvix Academy certificate.


7. FEES (INDIAN RUPEES, INR / ₹)

All students may be quoted these published fees. Two ways to pay: a one-time
payment at enrollment, or a monthly plan (auto-debited). The monthly plan
carries roughly a 15% premium to cover admin overhead.

FULL PIPELINE (Stages 1–3, 10 months)
  - One-time: ₹65,000
  - Monthly plan: ₹7,500 per month × 10 months = ₹75,000 total

STAGE 1 — FRONTEND (4 months)
  - One-time: ₹26,000
  - Monthly plan: ₹7,500 per month × 4 months = ₹30,000 total

STAGE 2 — BACKEND (4 months)
  - One-time: ₹26,000
  - Monthly plan: ₹7,500 per month × 4 months = ₹30,000 total

STAGE 3 — AGENTIC AI (2 months)
  - One-time: ₹15,000
  - Monthly plan: ₹7,500 per month × 2 months = ₹15,000 total

WHEN FEES ARE PAID
- One-time fees are paid at enrollment.
- Monthly fees are auto-debited.

FEE ADJUSTMENTS (quote only as below)

- Agentic AI capstone passed: no further course fees are due (all stages
  complete).

HOW TO PAY

- International students: Stripe Billing (recurring card subscription, with
  automated retry on failed payments).

INTERNATIONAL PRICING

  asks for their local price, the agent takes details and arranges a callback —
  it does NOT convert or invent a figure.


8. REFUND POLICY
----------------
- One-time payment: NO refund. This applies to the full pipeline and to any
  single stage.
- Monthly installments: if a student withdraws, the auto-debit is cancelled and
  the current month's installment (if already debited) PLUS all remaining
  months are refunded or not charged. Installments for completed months are NOT
  refunded.
- Method: refunds are returned to the original payment method via Razorpay or
  Stripe.
- Anything beyond this: offer an advisor callback.


9. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------
Q: How does the program work?
A: It is a 10-month, milestone-gated program in three stages — Frontend,
   Backend, then Agentic AI. Each stage ends in an evaluation you must clear to
   unlock the next stage and a matching 6-month paid internship. Strong
   internship performance leads to a full-time offer at Jadvix LTD.

Q: What happens if I fail a gate evaluation?
A: You stay in your current stage, keep attending coursework, and can retry
   after a 2-week cooling-off period, up to a maximum of 2 attempts. (The policy
   for what happens after both retries are used is still being finalised.)

Q: Can I keep studying if I don't get an internship offer?
A: Yes. Internship offers are capped by Jadvix LTD's confirmed intern capacity
   per cohort, so clearing an evaluation does not guarantee a place. You continue
   your coursework regardless, and clearing the gate keeps you eligible.

Q: What is your refund policy?
A: One-time payments are non-refundable. On the monthly plan, if you withdraw we
   cancel the auto-debit and refund (or do not charge) the current month plus all
   remaining months; completed months are not refunded. Refunds go back to your
   original payment method.

Q: How do I pay?
A: Indian students pay via Razorpay (UPI Autopay for monthly debits).
   International students pay via Stripe Billing (recurring card subscription).
   You can choose a one-time payment or a monthly plan.

Q: Is the program online or offline?
A: Both. Delivery is hybrid — online and offline — so you can take part from
   Chennai, Coimbatore, or remotely.

Q: What certificate do I get?
A: A UK-issued Jadvix Academy certificate. Students who pass the Agentic AI
   capstone and convert to full-time employment also receive a 1 gram gold coin.

Q: Who are the coordinators?
A: Each cohort has a Technical Coordinator (curriculum delivery, code reviews,
   gate-test design and evaluation) and an English Coordinator (professional
   communication and client-facing readiness).

Q: Will I definitely get a job at the end?
A: It is a direct pathway to employment, not a guarantee. It depends on clearing
   each evaluation and on Jadvix's confirmed intern capacity that cohort. An
   advisor can talk you through it.

Q: How much is the internship stipend?
A: It is paid, as per Jadvix's internship policy. The amount is not published —
   an advisor can discuss specifics.
