1. THE COMPANY
--------------
- Legal name: Jadvix LTD, a UK software company.
- Jadvix Academy is a Jadvix LTD product — its training arm.
- Locations: Ruislip, United Kingdom; Chennai, India; Coimbatore, India.
- Delivery of the course is run from Chennai and Coimbatore, with Jadvix LTD
  in the UK.


2. THE PROGRAM (OVERVIEW)
-------------------------
- One program: "Full Stack Development with Agentic AI".
- Format: a ten-month, milestone-gated, train-to-hire program.
- Delivery: hybrid — online and offline. A student can take part from Chennai,
  Coimbatore, or remotely.
- Three stages, in order:
    Stage 1, Frontend Development, months one to four.
    Stage 2, Backend Development, months five to eight.
    Stage 3, Agentic AI, months nine and ten.
- Each stage ends in an evaluation the student must clear to unlock the next
  stage. Clearing a stage also earns a matching six-month PAID internship.
- AI-assisted engineering with Claude Code runs through the whole program —
  it is built in, not an add-on.
- Learning is by building: guided, client-style project work and real
  code-review cycles, not passive lectures.
- Every cohort has two coordinators:
    Technical Coordinator — curriculum delivery, code reviews, gate-test
      design and evaluation.
    English Coordinator — professional communication, client-facing
      readiness, spoken and written English coaching.
- Certificate: a UK-issued Jadvix Academy certificate.

KEY STATS
- Ten months of structured training.
- Six months of paid internship, per stage cleared.
- Hybrid delivery, online and offline.
- A UK-issued Jadvix Academy certificate.


3. THE STAGES AND GATES
-----------------------
Stage 1, Frontend Development, months one to four
  Summary: core web, React, and AI-assisted engineering with Claude Code.
  Evaluation: the Frontend Evaluation, at the end of month four.
  On pass: a Frontend Development Intern offer, and cleared for Stage 2.
  On fail: continue Stage 1, and retry after two weeks. Maximum two attempts.

Stage 2, Backend Development, months five to eight
  Summary: server-side fundamentals, data, and full stack integration with
  the student's Stage 1 work.
  Evaluation: the Backend Evaluation, which has TWO parts. Both must be
  cleared:
    - A practical test: a timed, hands-on backend test set by the Technical
      Coordinator.
    - A full stack project: an individually assigned project, given to every
      student, integrating Stage 1 and Stage 2 work.
  When: the end of month eight.
  On pass: a Backend Development Intern offer, and cleared for Stage 3.
  On fail: continue Stage 2, and retry after two weeks. Maximum two attempts.

Stage 3, Agentic AI, months nine and ten
  Summary: LLM-powered agents, tool use, and shipping a production-style
  agentic capstone.
  Evaluation: the Agentic AI Capstone, at the end of month ten — a
  production-style agentic feature reviewed against Jadvix engineering
  standards.
  On pass: an Agentic AI Development Intern offer, for six months.
  On fail: continue Stage 3, and retry after two weeks. Maximum two attempts.

SEQUENTIAL RULE
- Every evaluation must be cleared to unlock the next stage.
- Backend cannot begin until the Frontend evaluation is cleared.
- Agentic AI cannot begin until the Backend evaluation is cleared, meaning
  both the practical test and the project.
- The same sequential rule applies to lateral entry, where someone joins for a
  single stage.
- Each pass unlocks the matching six-month paid internship.

RETRY POLICY
- A student who fails an evaluation stays in their current stage and keeps
  attending coursework.
- They may retry after a two-week cooling-off period.
- A maximum of two attempts.
- NOT YET DECIDED: what happens after both retry attempts are used up is still
  being finalised. The agent must NOT invent an answer — point them to the
  Contact page or contact@jadvix.uk.


4. CURRICULUM (WHAT IS TAUGHT)
------------------------------
Stage 1, Frontend, months one to four
  - Core Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript ES6 and later, responsive design,
    and the Git and GitHub workflow.
  - Modern Framework: React.js — components, hooks, state management, routing.
  - AI-Assisted Engineering: frontend enhancement and productivity workflows
    using Claude Code.
  - Applied Practice: guided client-style project builds and code-review
    cycles.

Stage 2, Backend, months five to eight
  - Server-Side Fundamentals: Node.js and Express or equivalent, REST API
    design, authentication and authorization.
  - Data: SQL and NoSQL design, query optimization, data modelling.
  - Integration: full stack integration with Stage 1 frontend projects, and
    deployment basics.
  - Applied Practice: end-to-end feature delivery under code review.
  - Evaluation Project: an individually assigned full stack project.

Stage 3, Agentic AI, months nine and ten
  - Agentic Systems: LLM-powered agents, tool use, workflow automation.
  - Applied AI Engineering: building and shipping agentic features into full
    stack apps.
  - Capstone: a production-style agentic feature reviewed against Jadvix
    engineering standards.

Technologies covered across the program: HTML and CSS, JavaScript, React,
Node.js, REST APIs, SQL and NoSQL, Git and GitHub, Claude Code, Agentic AI,
and full stack development.

Supporting tracks, across all stages: the Technical Coordinator track and the
English Coordinator track. See section two.


5. INTERNSHIP
-------------
- Length: six months per internship, one per stage cleared.
- Stipend: it is PAID, as per Jadvix's internship policy. DO NOT state an
  amount — the amount is intentionally not published.
- Work: interns work on live or client-adjacent projects, under the
  supervision of the Technical Coordinator and an assigned engineering lead.
- Parallel coursework: Frontend and Backend interns may continue coursework in
  parallel with their internship.
- Terms: interns sign standard confidentiality and IP assignment terms before
  starting.
- Capacity: internship offers are capped by Jadvix LTD's confirmed intern
  capacity each cohort. Clearing an evaluation keeps a student eligible but
  does NOT guarantee a place.

INTERN ROLES BY STAGE
- Clearing Stage 1 leads to a Frontend Development Intern offer.
- Clearing Stage 2 leads to a Backend Development Intern offer.
- Clearing Stage 3 leads to an Agentic AI Development Intern offer.


6. THE PATHWAY TO EMPLOYMENT (IMPORTANT WORDING)
------------------------------------------------
- The program is "a direct pathway to full-time employment at Jadvix LTD"
  after the six-month internship.
- It is subject to clearing each evaluation, AND to Jadvix LTD's confirmed
  intern capacity each cohort.
- This is NEVER a guaranteed job. The agent must always describe it as a
  pathway, never a guarantee.
- Conversion to full-time, across all three tracks, is based on code-review
  scores, sprint and task completion rate, reliability, and coordinator
  sign-off — leading to a full-time offer at Jadvix LTD.

COMPLETION REWARD
- Students who pass the Agentic AI capstone AND convert to full-time
  employment receive a one gram gold coin and a UK-issued Jadvix Academy
  certificate.

8. REFUND POLICY
----------------
- One-time payment: no refund. This applies to the full pipeline and to any
  single stage.
- Monthly instalments: if a student withdraws, the auto-debit is cancelled,
  and the current month's instalment, if it has already been debited, plus all
  remaining months, are refunded or not charged. Instalments for months
  already completed are NOT refunded.
- Method: refunds are returned to the original payment method, through
  Razorpay or Stripe.
- Anything beyond this: point them to the Refund Policy page or the Contact
  page.


9. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------
Q: How does the program work?
A: It is a ten-month, milestone-gated program in three stages — Frontend,
   Backend, then Agentic AI. Each stage ends in an evaluation you must clear to
   unlock the next stage and a matching six-month paid internship. Strong
   internship performance leads to a full-time offer at Jadvix LTD.

Q: What does the full course cost?
A: The full pipeline is sixty-five thousand rupees as a one-time payment, or
   seven thousand five hundred rupees a month for ten months, which comes to
   seventy-five thousand rupees. The Fees page has every option and a
   calculator.

Q: Can I join just one stage?
A: Yes. Frontend is twenty-six thousand rupees one-time, Backend is twenty-six
   thousand rupees one-time, and Agentic AI is fifteen thousand rupees
   one-time. The same sequential rule applies: each evaluation must be cleared
   before going on to the next stage.

Q: What happens if I fail a gate evaluation?
A: You stay in your current stage, keep attending coursework, and can retry
   after a two-week cooling-off period, up to a maximum of two attempts. The
   policy for what happens after both retries are used is still being
   finalised.

Q: Can I keep studying if I don't get an internship offer?
A: Yes. Internship offers are capped by Jadvix LTD's confirmed intern capacity
   each cohort, so clearing an evaluation does not guarantee a place. You
   continue your coursework regardless, and clearing the gate keeps you
   eligible.

Q: What is your refund policy?
A: One-time payments are non-refundable. On the monthly plan, if you withdraw
   we cancel the auto-debit and refund, or do not charge, the current month
   plus all remaining months. Months already completed are not refunded.
   Refunds go back to your original payment method.

Q: How do I pay?
A: Indian students pay through Razorpay, with UPI Autopay for monthly debits.
   International students pay through Stripe Billing, as a recurring card
   subscription. You can choose a one-time payment or a monthly plan.

Q: Is the program online or offline?
A: Both. Delivery is hybrid, online and offline, so you can take part from
   Chennai, Coimbatore, or remotely.

Q: What certificate do I get?
A: A UK-issued Jadvix Academy certificate. Students who pass the Agentic AI
   capstone and convert to full-time employment also receive a one gram gold
   coin.

Q: Who are the coordinators?
A: Each cohort has a Technical Coordinator, who handles curriculum delivery,
   code reviews, and gate-test design and evaluation, and an English
   Coordinator, for professional communication and client-facing readiness.

Q: Will I definitely get a job at the end?
A: It is a direct pathway to employment, not a guarantee. It depends on
   clearing each evaluation and on Jadvix's confirmed intern capacity that
   cohort. The team can talk it through with you — the Contact page has the
   details.

Q: How much is the internship stipend?
A: It is paid, as per Jadvix's internship policy. The amount is not published.
   The team can discuss specifics through the Contact page.
